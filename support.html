MISSING: PUN Support

Help with the iPhone, iPad, and web versions

How do I play?

Search the house for keys, hide from Pun, and reach the exit. On iPhone or iPad, use the left stick to move and run, drag the right side to look, and tap USE to interact, hide, or escape.

Multiplayer will not connect

Check that every player has internet access and has entered the same room code. If a room stalls, leave it and create a fresh room. Solo mode remains available without a connection.

Why is voice chat missing on iOS?

Proximity voice chat is not included in the first App Store release. Multiplayer gameplay still works. The browser version retains its optional voice feature.

Report a problem

Open a GitHub support issue and include your device model, iOS version, whether you were playing solo or multiplayer, and what happened immediately before the problem.
