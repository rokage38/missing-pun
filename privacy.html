Privacy Policy

MISSING: PUN · Effective 28 August 2026

MISSING: PUN is an independent game published by Rowan Amin. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or tracking.

Information used by the game

Solo play runs on your device. If you choose multiplayer, the nickname you enter, a randomly generated session identifier, a room code, and live gameplay state are transmitted through Supabase Realtime so players in the room can play together. This information is used only to provide the live match and is not intentionally retained by the developer as a player profile.

The iOS version does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifier. Voice chat is not available in the first iOS release.

Browser version

The web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. It offers optional proximity voice chat. If you explicitly turn that feature on and grant microphone permission, audio is sent directly between players using WebRTC; it is not recorded or stored by the developer. Supabase carries connection-signalling messages, and standard internet connectivity services may process network addresses to establish the peer-to-peer connection.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the public website and Supabase provides live multiplayer infrastructure. Like most online services, these providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and connection logs under their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Supabase Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can play solo without sending multiplayer game data. You can remove local game data by clearing the website's storage or deleting the app. Multiplayer rooms are temporary and end when players leave.

Children

The game is not directed to young children. It contains horror themes, violence, blood, and strong language.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
